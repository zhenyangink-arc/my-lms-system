# Management study-abroad modules

- Route: `/[space]/dashboard/admin/apps/study-abroad/universities`, `/documents`, `/visa`, `/analytics` (the shared insight component also serves `/records`)
- Audience: Management
- Archetype: Collection (`universities`, `documents`, `visa`); Operations overview (`analytics`); Audit / history (`records`)
- Primary job: Search, compare, and maintain university reference data; review application materials; monitor visa cases and tasks; identify service workload and trace recent service activity.
- Primary action: Create or maintain university data in `universities`; open the relevant student application or visa case in `documents` and `visa`; review actionable workload in `analytics`; inspect recent immutable activity in `records`.
- Information hierarchy: Shared application-module header → module notice or permission context → KPI summary → search/filter/view controls where applicable → collection table or actionable summary → pagination/footer or audit detail.
- Layout and density: Neutral Operations Workspace; full-width, compact management tables with horizontal overflow at narrow widths; stable solid surfaces; metric strips remain text-first and must not become decorative charts.
- Special components: University requirements workspace, document-review collection, visa-case and visa-task collections, platform-safe anonymous tenant summaries, analytics KPI definition lists, and recent-service audit table.
- Allowed deviations: Platform scope replaces student-level rows with anonymous institution aggregates. Analytics may use text metrics without a graphical chart because the primary task is workload scanning; every metric retains its visible label, value, and written summary. Records is capped at the 100 most recent events by the existing business rule.
- Accessibility risks: Search/filter controls need persistent labels and visible focus; sortable headers need keyboard operation plus `aria-sort`; wide tables need correct header associations; status must remain understandable without color; query failure, loading, and valid empty results must be distinct; destructive visa deletion requires a titled confirmation dialog and focus restoration; metric or chart-like summaries require equivalent text; platform summaries must not expose student data.
- Acceptance criteria: No raw Hex/RGB or `--app-*` tokens in the four module content components; all module branches expose distinct failure and valid-empty states; university failed subqueries do not render misleading empty collections; collection sorting and controls are keyboard accessible in their table implementations; service-record statuses use readable text plus a decorative icon; analytics metrics are represented as labeled definition-list data and a written summary; platform tables include captions and scoped headers; visa deletion remains behind confirmation; both `analytics` and `records` branches are covered.
