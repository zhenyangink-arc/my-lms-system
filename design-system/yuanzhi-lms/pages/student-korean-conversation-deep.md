# Korean conversation practice deep pages

- Route: `/dashboard/conversation-practice/[scenarioId]`, `/dashboard/conversation-practice/course`, `/dashboard/conversation-practice/ai-experience`, `/dashboard/conversation-practice/ai-experience/quick`, and `/dashboard/conversation-practice/ai-experience/practice` (also rendered through the canonical Korean app routes under `/[space]/apps/korean/`).
- Audience: Student; authorized staff may receive a read-only scenario preview.
- Archetype: Practice detail / conversation flow, with a small practice-collection entry view.
- Primary job: Choose a speaking format or scenario, complete a Korean conversation, review supporting expressions, and record a reflection.
- Primary action: Start or continue one conversation practice; in a live AI session, send the next text, image, or voice turn.
- Information hierarchy: Parent return path → scenario or practice-mode context → task/configuration → opaque conversation or reading workspace → status and recovery feedback → finish/reflection actions.
- Layout and density: Comfortable student density. Scenario reading content uses a bounded two-column layout at wide widths. AI sessions use one dominant opaque conversation surface with an optional narrow quick-prompt rail. Transcript, composer, supporting reading content, and reflection form remain opaque; glass is reserved for shell chrome outside this archetype.
- Special components: `ConversationAiExperience`, `FormalConversationPractice`, `PracticeReflectionForm`, native fieldsets and form controls, press-and-hold microphone control, conversation log, async status regions, and Next.js `Link` exit paths.
- Allowed deviations: Quick and formal AI practice share one conversation composition and therefore use one specification. Quick practice exposes text/voice/image and reply-language selectors and does not save a practice summary. Formal practice adds a required microphone check, scenario/difficulty/duration/reply configuration, elapsed-time header, explicit finish action, session restoration, and completion summary. The course route may use a card collection before entering the same scenario-detail composition.
- Accessibility risks: Microphone permission denial and unsupported recording/audio APIs; press-and-hold parity for pointer and keyboard; streaming reply announcements; focus visibility in dense mode selectors; selected state announcement; field-level reflection errors; transcript auto-scroll; and ensuring status is not conveyed by color alone.
- Acceptance criteria: Shell heading hierarchy is preserved with page headings at H2 or below; every interactive element has a visible keyboard focus indicator; icon-only controls have accessible names and decorative icons are hidden; selectors expose pressed state; AI start, connection, request, microphone-permission, empty, not-found, and data-error states explain recovery; every AI route has a keyboard-reachable return or finish path; transcript/reading/form surfaces are opaque; reflection fields have persistent labels with nearby errors; and audited runtime files contain no raw hex/RGB(A) colors or `--app-*` tokens.
